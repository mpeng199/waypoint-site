I need food.

Food pantries, hot meals you can walk into, groceries, and help signing up for SNAP — what most people still call food stamps. Nearly all of it is free, and most of it does not ask about immigration status.

24 places on this page. Nearly all are free. Most do not ask about immigration status. You can call them yourself — you do not have to go through us.

If you need help right now

  • 911You are in danger, or someone is badly hurt
  • 988You feel unsafe with yourself, or you need to talk to somebody now
  • 800-621-4673Someone at home or a partner is hurting you or frightening you
  • 311Anything else at all. Free, any hour, in your language

Who we are, and what we will never do

We are trained student volunteers. We help you find the free programs and professionals in New York that handle medical bills and insurance denials. We are not doctors, lawyers, benefits counselors, or insurance experts. We do not read your bills, fill out your forms, or tell you what you qualify for. We connect you to people who do that, and they do it for free. We never charge for anything.

This is printed on everything we hand out, and said out loud at every table.
