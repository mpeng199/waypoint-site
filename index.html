The help is already here.

New York is full of free health care. Most of the people who need it never reach the door.

Join the corps →

All around them. Out of reach.

Senior centers, counselors, clinics, Medicare help, food, transit. All free. All quietly going unused, because no one walks people to it.

So we became the thread.

Waypoint is a trained student volunteer corps. We carry older, low-income, and immigrant New Yorkers to the care that is already theirs.

Find what's already yours.

At libraries and senior centers, students help neighbors find the free services they qualify for and leave with one clear next step. When a certified counselor is there, they handle the rest.

So no one sits alone.

Through trusted partners, vetted students show up on a schedule, in groups and supervised pairs. Games, music, reading, company that keeps coming back.

We share the way. We never play the doctor.

We are students, not doctors, nurses, social workers, or benefits counselors. We share information, never medical or legal advice. And we never charge for anything.

Now, pull a thread.

Wherever you stand, there is a way to help carry someone to the care that is waiting for them. The journey keeps going from here.

For students

For the students who show up.

Join a trained corps and spend your hours on something real. We onboard you, a partner adds their training, and you join a semester cohort with a schedule. At events you greet, listen, translate, and walk people to the right professional. You inform and refer; the regulated work stays with certified counselors. Multilingual students especially.
