Privacy & legal.

Plain language about what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

The short version. We are a student-led volunteer initiative. We only collect what you choose to send us through a form or by email, we use it only to reply to you, we never sell it, and we use no cookies or trackers. We are not doctors, lawyers, benefits counselors, or insurance experts, we never take custody of your bills or paperwork, and we never charge for anything.

Who we are

Waypoint is a student-led volunteer initiative based in New York City that connects residents to the free programs and professionals in New York that handle medical bills and insurance denials, and to other free and low-cost health resources that already exist. You can reach us about anything on this page at waypointoutreach@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us, either through a form or by email:

  • Volunteering (students): your name, email, school and grade, the languages you speak, and any message you add.
  • Partnerships: your name, organization, email, what you told us you can offer, and your message.
  • Email to us: if you write to waypointoutreach@gmail.com, we keep your email address and whatever you put in the message, in that mailbox, until we no longer need it to answer you.

We do not ask for medical information, immigration or public-charge status, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal data, and we ask that you do not send them through these forms or by email.

The same applies to anything to do with a bill or a claim. We do not ask for, and you should not send us, medical bills, statements, explanation-of-benefits or denial letters, insurance cards, account or medical record numbers, or income documents. At an event our volunteers take custody of no documents at all: they do not accept paper, photograph anything, transcribe it, or hold it for a second look, and they never record a name alongside any medical or financial detail. The counts we keep are anonymous and aggregate, such as how many people asked about a hospital bill.

Why we collect it

We use what you send for one purpose: to follow up with you about volunteering or a partnership. That is all.

What we never do

  • We set no cookies and use no analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We never build advertising profiles or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Third parties

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, it contacts Google's servers, and Google may receive your IP address as part of delivering them. We use Google Fonts only to display type, for nothing else.

When you submit a form, it is stored on our behalf by Supabase, a hosted database provider, on servers in the United States. Supabase processes the submission only to store it for us and does not sell your data. We also store your IP address alongside your submission solely for rate-limiting (to prevent automated spam); it is not used for advertising or profiling and is deleted along with your submission when you ask us to remove your data.

When you submit a form, we send ourselves an email notification via Resend, a transactional email service. Resend receives your name, email address, and any other fields you submitted in order to deliver that notification. Resend does not sell your data.

Minors and parental consent

Some of our volunteers are minors. Students under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent before taking part, and we collect only the minimum needed to follow up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we will delete it promptly.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send only as long as we need it to follow up and to run the program, and then we delete it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your choices

Email us at waypointoutreach@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete the information you have sent us, or to ask us not to contact you again. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; we will honor those requests.

Not medical or legal advice

We are trained student volunteers, not doctors, lawyers, benefits counselors, or insurance experts. We share information, never medical, legal, insurance, or billing advice, and we never charge for anything. We do not read your bills, fill out your forms, or tell you what you qualify for. Anything on this site is general information, not professional advice, and not a substitute for speaking with a qualified professional.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date below.

Effective June 2026. Questions: waypointoutreach@gmail.com
